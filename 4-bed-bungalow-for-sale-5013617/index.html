About the property

Description property

Situated just 3 kilometres from the vibrant town of Westport along the Castlebar Road, this well-presented four-bedroom detached bungalow offers the perfect balance of peaceful countryside living and everyday convenience.

Set on a mature site extending to approximately 0.86 acres, the property enjoys an attractive private setting with spacious gardens, excellent parking and a substantial detached chalet and garage.

The main residence itself extends to approximately 115.93 sq.m. (1,247.8 sq.ft.). The property has been carefully maintained and is presented in excellent condition throughout.

Recently redecorated, this home offers bright, fresh interiors and is ready for immediate occupation.

This well-proportioned home comprises of four generously sized bedrooms, including a master bedroom with en-suite, together with a fully tiled family bathroom, a welcoming entrance hall, a cosy living room featuring a modern electric stove, an impressive open-plan kitchen, dining and family area complete with a fitted kitchen and a solid fuel stove with back boiler, with a separate utility room and storage room attached to add further practicality.

This inviting space is ideal for both family living and entertaining.

Outside, the mature gardens to the front and rear provide excellent privacy and ample space for outdoor enjoyment.

A gravel driveway offers generous off-street parking, while additional storage and fuel sheds enhance the property's functionality.

A standout feature is the detached one bed self-contained Chalet extending to approximately 51.95 sqm. (559.18 sq ft), complete with electricity and plumbing. Offering excellent versatility to stay as a one bed chalet or potential conversion into a home office or studio.

Additionally, there is a separate garage (15.4sqm/165.76 sq ft), store (8.4sqm/90.41sqft) and boiler house (2 sqm/21.52 sq ft).

Combining spacious accommodation, a generous site and an enviable location just minutes from Westport, this is an excellent opportunity to acquire a quality family home in one of County Mayo's most sought-after areas.
